# Update log

## 2026-11-08

* **Services tab as a table** (playtest, user design): the tab is always a Taxation-style table (city-only with District service budgets off) (Type | Budget / Fee | Estimate, fee sub-rows, reset buttons and markers, NET per service and ESTIMATED TOTAL) in place of vanilla's list, detail and info column; a district view sets the area's budget, shows the city's fees read-only and the district's NET (fee share by usage, `districtServiceFees`, minus its upkeep). Closes #62. [impl/district-service-budgets.md](impl/district-service-budgets.md#ui)
* **Taxation tab polish** (playtest): "Density tax" renamed "Density" and drawn after Education/Resources (sections have a main/additional kind); a district view shows its own estimates (`DistrictTaxEstimateUISystem`); every estimate is booked as charged (`PayerTaxParts`: each payer's clamped density part at its own rates); "Use city rates" replaced by a reset button and target marker on every row (`UI/src/common/`); Outside Districts sets its own tax rates (save format 3). [impl/tax-engine-ui.md](impl/tax-engine-ui.md).
* **One district picker in the Economy panel header**: at the left of the tabs, shown on Budget, Taxation and Services by each tab's own rule and hidden on Production and Loans; a divider separates [Budget | Taxation | Services] from [Production | Loans]; the per-tab pickers are gone (Taxation keeps "Use city rates"); the city option is the city's name (`toolbarBottom.cityName`, "Entire City" while blank). [impl/budget-chart.md § District selector](impl/budget-chart.md#district-selector).

* **Every tax effect on one rate computation**: demand, happiness and home choice take each home's rate from `TaxRateResolver` (`ResidentRate`: the city's rate for the resident's education level plus the district's difference at that level only plus the density tax), so a district's Highly Educated override moves only highly educated residents; the Taxation estimate takes each payer's base at its own resolved rate, so a district's density override no longer skews the city cell; `districts` is published while the tax engine charges; the Taxation district picker is one component (`TaxDistrictBar`). `impl/tax-engine.md` split into the engine, [impl/tax-engine-effects.md](impl/tax-engine-effects.md) and [impl/tax-engine-ui.md](impl/tax-engine-ui.md).

## 2026-11-07

* **Economy batch 4 landed** (PRs #63-#76): Economy tabs reordered (Budget, Taxation, Services, Production, Loans); per-district service budgets with the Services tab selector and slider, the Budget tab following them, and a per-service Service Upkeep breakdown; one district selector shared across tabs; tax rates per save; the tax engine charging density tax as a real tax behind a game-version guard and the Simulation changes master switch, with demand, happiness and home choice reacting; density rows with zoning icons in vanilla row layout; the Options feature toggles reordered ([impl/tax-engine.md](impl/tax-engine.md), [impl/district-service-budgets.md](impl/district-service-budgets.md), [impl/budget-chart.md](impl/budget-chart.md)).
* **Tax home choice**: households score homes with the tax term at each home's charged rate (density + district adjustments) through `TaxFindPropertySystem`, a copy of `HouseholdFindPropertySystem`'s update that runs exactly while the tax engine does ([impl/tax-engine-effects.md § Where people move](impl/tax-engine-effects.md#where-people-move)).
* **Tax happiness**: citizens' tax happiness follows their home's charged rate (density + district adjustments) through `TaxHappinessSystem`, a copy of `CitizenHappinessSystem`'s update that runs exactly while the tax engine does ([impl/tax-engine-effects.md § Happiness](impl/tax-engine-effects.md#happiness)).
* **District taxes: each district sets its own rates** (user decision, replacing the ±10 adjustment on the city's): a district may override any zone, education-level, resource or density rate; the engine charges the payer's vanilla rate plus the district-minus-city difference for what it earns from, plus its density tax, which is now its own tax at −5..+10. The table is keyed by area (city or district entity), save format 2 drops the old district adjustments. One shared effective-rate computation, `TaxRateResolver`, for the demand, happiness and home-choice slices ([impl/tax-engine.md](impl/tax-engine.md#the-rate-model)).
